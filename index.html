DAYWORK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Daywork ("the app") is operated by Premier Staffing Solutions LLC ("we", "us"), a yacht-crew staffing company in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your choices are.

What we collect

  • Account details — email address, name, phone number, and the role you choose (crew or captain). Your phone number is private: it is never shown to other users and is used only for account and support purposes.
  • Profile information you provide — headline, home marina, city, certifications you list (e.g. STCW, ENG1), and, for captains, vessel details.
  • Photos you choose to upload — a profile photo, and photos of certificates if you want them verified. The app only sees the photos you pick; it does not read the rest of your photo library.
  • ID verification — one photo of a government ID. It is stored privately and seen only by Premier Staffing Solutions to confirm you are a real person. It is never shown to other users.
  • Documents you upload — for crew, an optional CV (PDF or Word). It can be opened only by you, by captains using the app, and by us.
  • Marketplace activity — dayworks you post or apply to, notes you add to an application, bookings, completed dayworks, and ratings and reviews.
  • Messages — chats between crew and captains are stored so both sides can see their conversation history.
  • Payments — when a captain books, payment is taken in the app. When crew set up payouts, they give their payout details. Card numbers, bank details and the identity checks needed for payouts are collected and held by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not see or store full card or bank numbers. We keep a record of each payment: amount, date, status, and who paid whom.
  • Notifications — if you turn on alerts, we store a push token for your device so we can send them.
  • Crash reports — if the app hits an error, a technical report is sent to our error-reporting service. Reports are set up to leave out names, emails and IP addresses.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or browsing history. We do not show ads and do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: showing dayworks, connecting crew with captains, bookings, payments, chat, and reputation.
  • To keep the community safe: verifying IDs and certificates, and reviewing reports of abusive behavior.
  • To fix bugs and keep the app stable.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Who helps us run the app

These companies process data for us, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase (hosted on AWS, United States) — database, sign-in, file storage
  • Stripe — payments and payouts
  • Expo, Apple and Google — delivering push notifications
  • Sentry — crash reports

What other users see

Captains see the profile, ratings, certifications and CV of crew who apply to their dayworks. Crew see the captain's name, rating and vessel on a daywork. Your phone number, email, ID photo and payment details are never shown to other users.

Where it lives and how it's protected

Data is stored in the United States and sent over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so users only see what the app is meant to show them.

Your choices

  • Edit — edit your profile and certifications in the app at any time, and replace or remove your CV or photo.
  • Notifications — turn alerts off in your phone's settings.
  • Delete — permanently delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or by email. Steps and details: how to delete your account
  • Report / block — report or block another user from any chat.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, CV, posts, applications and messages are deleted. Records of completed payments are kept by us and by Stripe for as long as tax, accounting and anti-fraud law requires.

Age

Daywork is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it.
