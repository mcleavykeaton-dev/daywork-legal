DAYWORK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Daywork is a marketplace where yacht crew find day work and captains book them. It is operated by Premier Staffing Solutions LLC (“PSS”, “we”, “us”), a yacht-crew staffing company in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

This policy covers the Daywork app on iPhone and Android. It tells you what we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to get it deleted.

The short version

  • We collect what the marketplace needs to work: your account, your profile, your dayworks, your messages, and records of payments.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location, your contacts, or your browsing history.
  • Your phone number, email, ID photo and payment details are never shown to other users.
  • You can delete your account, and the data that goes with it, at any time.

The app only receives the photos and files you choose. It does not read the rest of your photo library or your files.

Payments

Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe. Card numbers, bank and debit card details, and the identity checks Stripe requires for payouts are given directly to Stripe and held by Stripe. We never see or store full card or bank numbers. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Crash reports

Crash reports contain technical details about the error and the device, such as the app version and phone model. They are set up to leave out names, email addresses and IP addresses.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, calendar, health data, browsing history, or microphone audio. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking tools, and we do not build advertising profiles.

How we use your data

  • To run the marketplace: showing dayworks, handling applications and bookings, moving payments, and delivering chat and alerts.
  • To build trust: showing ratings, completed dayworks and verified certifications.
  • To keep people safe: verifying IDs, reviewing reports, and acting on abuse or fraud.
  • To support you when you contact us.
  • To fix bugs and keep the app reliable.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell it or rent it.

Who sees your data

Other users. Captains see the profile, photo, ratings, certifications and CV of crew who apply to their dayworks. Crew see a captain's name, photo, rating and vessel on a daywork. Chat messages are seen by the two people in the chat. Your phone number, email address, ID photo and payment details are never shown to other users.

Premier Staffing Solutions. As the operator, we can see account and marketplace data in order to run the service, verify IDs and certificates, give support, and review reports.

Legal reasons. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or of PSS.

A change of ownership. If Daywork or PSS is sold or merged, your data may move to the new owner, who must keep honoring this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open.

When you delete your account, we delete your account details, profile, photos, ID photo, certifications, CV, posts, applications and messages.

We keep records of completed payments after an account is deleted, for as long as tax, accounting and anti-fraud law requires. Stripe keeps its own records under its own policy. We may also keep reports of abusive behavior to keep the community safe.

How we protect it

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections.
  • Sign-in sessions are stored in your phone's secure storage.
  • Database rules limit each user to the data the app is meant to show them.
  • ID photos, CVs and certificate photos are kept in private storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct. View and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Remove a photo or CV. Replace or remove them from your Profile tab.
  • Turn off alerts. Use your phone's notification settings.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. If you can't get into the app, email us and we will delete it within 30 days. Full steps: how to delete your account
  • Ask for a copy, or ask a question. Email us at the address below.
  • Report or block another user from any chat.

If a daywork still has money held for it, complete or cancel it before you delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under your local privacy law, such as the right to know what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us to use any of them. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

Age

Daywork is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has made an account, email us and we will remove it.

Where your data is kept

Daywork is offered in the United States, and data is stored in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will note it in the app and update the date at the top. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
