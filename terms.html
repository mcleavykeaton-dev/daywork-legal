How Daywork works

Captains post a daywork with the vessel, place, date, tasks and day rate. Crew apply. The captain chooses who to book.

Crew apply to dayworks, and can add a note. If booked, you are expected to show up on time and do the work described.

Booking is the commitment. When a captain books a crew member, the captain pays in the app and a chat opens between the two of you.

Completion. When the work is done, the captain marks the daywork complete. That releases the payment to the crew, and both sides can rate each other.

If a captain does not mark a daywork complete or raise a problem within 3 days after the daywork date, we release the payment to the crew.

Cancellations and refunds

If the captain cancels before the day of the daywork: the captain is refunded in full, minus a 3.5% card processing fee.

If the crew member cancels: the captain is refunded 100%. The cancellation is counted on the crew member's profile, where captains can see it.

If the captain cancels on the day of the daywork: the crew member is paid 50% of the day rate and the rest is refunded to the captain. If the captain cancels after the work has started: the crew member is paid in full.

If a crew member does not show up: the captain is refunded 100%. The no-show is counted on the crew member's profile. Two no-shows and the account is suspended.

Problems with the work. If you believe the work was not done as agreed, or you did the work and were not paid, contact us within 3 days of the daywork date at the email below. We will look at the chat history and what both sides tell us, and decide whether the payment is released, refunded or split. Our decision on the held payment is final.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show them.
