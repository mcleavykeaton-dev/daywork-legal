Delete your account

Daywork · Premier Staffing Solutions LLC · Last updated: September 29, 2026

You can delete your Daywork account and its data at any time. Daywork is operated by Premier Staffing Solutions LLC.

Delete it in the app

  • Open Daywork and sign in.
  • Go to the Profile tab, then Settings.
  • Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete it by email

If you can't get into the app, email mcleavykeaton@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Daywork account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile, photo, certifications, and CV
  • Dayworks you posted and applications you sent
  • Your messages

What we may keep

  • Payment records — records of completed payments are kept by us and by our payment processor, Stripe, for as long as tax, accounting, and anti-fraud law requires.
  • Reports — reports of abusive behavior may be kept to keep the community safe.

Before you delete

If a daywork you booked or were booked for still has money held for it, complete or cancel that daywork first. The app will tell you if this applies.

Questions

Email mcleavykeaton@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
